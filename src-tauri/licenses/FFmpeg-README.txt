StreamWave bundles FFmpeg (ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe) as separate, unmodified programs.
Build: BtbN FFmpeg-Builds, "ffmpeg-n8.1-latest-win64-lgpl-8.1" (LGPL variant, distributed under LGPL v3, no GPL components).
Source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html and https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds

You may replace ffmpeg.exe / ffprobe.exe next to StreamWave.exe with any other build.
